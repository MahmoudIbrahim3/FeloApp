اللي بيفضل على جهازك

اللي بيخرج من جهازك وليه

  1. تحديث الكتالوج. التطبيق بينزّل قايمة الفيديوهات اللي راجعناها من السيرفر بتاعنا (على Supabase في وسط أوروبا (فرانكفورت)). الطلبات دي مفيهاش أي معلومة عنك أو عن ولادك.
  2. بلاغات من غير أسماء. لما تخبّي فيديو أو فيديو يبطّل يشتغل، التطبيق بيبعت لنا رقم الفيديو على YouTube والسبب بس (زي "الأهل خبّوه"). مفيش أي رقم للجهاز أو للمستخدم أو للطفل، فمستحيل نعرف البلاغ جاي من مين. بنحتفظ بالبلاغات 12 شهر علشان نحسّن الكتالوج، وبعدين بنمسحها.
  3. الفيديوهات. الفيديوهات بتشتغل في المشغّل الرسمي المدمج بتاع YouTube، في وضع الخصوصية المحسّن. فيلو بيستخدم خدمات YouTube API. وقت تشغيل الفيديو، YouTube (جوجل) بيوصله اللي بيوصل لأي مشغّل ويب، زي عنوان الـIP ونوع الجهاز والمتصفح، وممكن يعرض إعلاناته غير المخصّصة على فيديوهات الأطفال. إحنا بلّغنا جوجل إن فيلو موجّه للأطفال. استخدام YouTube للمعلومات دي خاضع لسياسة خصوصية جوجل (https://policies.google.com/privacy)، وباستخدامك لفيلو إنت موافق على شروط خدمة YouTube (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).
  4. الاشتراك (اختياري). كل الفيديوهات في فيلو مجانية. الاشتراك الاختياري بيضيف مميزات للأهل: أطفال أكتر، وسجل مشاهدة الأسبوع كله. الشراء بيتم عن طريق أبل (App Store) أو جوجل (Google Play) بحسابك في المتجر، وإحنا عمرنا ما بنشوف بيانات الدفع. علشان نعرف الاشتراك شغال ولا لأ بنستخدم RevenueCat (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)، وبيوصله رقم عشوائي بيعمله التطبيق، وإيصال الشراء، والباقة اللي اشتريتها، ومعلومات بسيطة عن التطبيق ونظام التشغيل. التطبيق مش بيكلّم RevenueCat غير بعد ما توافق جوه التطبيق، ومن قسم الأهل بس (أو علشان يتأكد من اشتراك وصل لميعاد تجديده). مش بنبعت لـ RevenueCat أي أسامي أو إيميلات أو بيانات تانية.

Feelo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Feelo is made by Lujain Integrated Solutions, Egypt ("we"). Contact: feelokids@gmail.com.

The short version. Feelo has no accounts, no ads from us, and no analytics or tracking. Your children's names, ages, settings and viewing history stay on your device. We never sell data.

What stays on your device

The nickname, avatar color and age group you enter for each child; their daily limit and allowed collections; which videos they watched, liked or that you hid; and your parent PIN, stored only as a salted, one-way hash in the device's secure storage. None of this is sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What leaves your device, and why

  1. Catalog updates. The app downloads the list of reviewed videos from our server (hosted by Supabase in Central EU (Frankfurt)). These requests contain no information about you or your children.
  2. Anonymous video reports. When you hide a video, or a video stops working, the app sends us only the video's YouTube ID and the reason (for example "hidden by a parent"). No device, user or child identifier is included, so a report can't be linked to you. We keep reports for 12 months to improve the catalog, then delete them.
  3. Videos. Videos play in YouTube's official embedded player, in privacy-enhanced mode. Feelo uses YouTube API Services. When a video plays, YouTube (Google) receives the information any web player receives, such as your IP address and device/browser details, and may show its own non-personalized ads on Made for Kids videos. We have told Google that Feelo is directed to children. YouTube's use of this information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy, and by using Feelo you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
  4. Subscriptions (optional). Every video in Feelo is free. The optional subscription adds parent features: more child profiles and the weekly activity report. Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) with your store account; we never see your payment details. To check whether a subscription is active we use RevenueCat, which receives a random app-generated ID, your purchase receipt, the product bought and basic app/OS information. The app contacts RevenueCat only after you agree to it in the app, and only from the parent section (or to check a subscription that has reached its renewal date). We don't send RevenueCat any names, emails or other attributes.

Children

Feelo is designed for children aged 3–12 to use under a parent's supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Anything a parent enters about a child stays on the device. Purchases, settings and links that leave the app are behind the parent PIN.

Retention and deletion

On-device data: until you delete it in the app or uninstall. Video reports: 12 months. RevenueCat purchase records: as long as needed to provide the subscription, or until you ask us to delete them. Email feelokids@gmail.com with your purchase receipt ID and we'll delete them within 30 days. Catalog data from YouTube is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as YouTube requires.

Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. The PIN is never stored in plain text. Access to our server is restricted to the catalog and anonymous reports.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete data. Since we hold nothing that identifies you, most requests are handled on your device. For anything else, contact feelokids@gmail.com.

Changes

We'll update this page and the date above, and show a notice in the app's parent section for important changes.
